Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

What does the LTM Security Platform assess?

The platform evaluates your Palo Alto firewall estate across 44+ controls covering software & platform currency, capacity, security services, networking, VPN, administration, and logging & monitoring.

How does the AI copilot work?

The AI Workspace connects to your firewall data through an agent endpoint. Ask questions in plain English and the copilot returns data-driven answers with remediation guidance.

Is my data stored securely?

Yes. Assessment history, conversations, and reports are persisted in your own PostgreSQL database or secure storage, never shared.

Can I export reports?

Absolutely. Generate executive summaries (PDF) and detailed assessment workbooks (XLSX), all stored in your Reports history.
